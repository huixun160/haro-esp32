# Rule: Pitfall Log Required

All resolved platform bugs, debugging insights, and non-obvious workarounds must create an entry in `AIOS/docs/pitfalls/`.

Each pitfall document must include:
- **Date** of discovery
- **Symptom** — what went wrong or was confusing
- **Root Cause** — why it happened
- **Fix / Workaround** — how it was resolved
- **Affected Module** — which module(s) were impacted
- **Prevention** — how to avoid this in the future

This rule ensures engineering knowledge is not lost between sessions or engineers.
